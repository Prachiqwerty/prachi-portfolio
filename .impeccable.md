## Design Context

### Users
Portfolio reviewers, design leaders, and hiring teams evaluating a product designer's ability to diagnose complex learning experiences and translate an AI-native curriculum into clear product systems. They need to understand the problem, evidence, reasoning, and outcome quickly.

### Brand Personality
Editorial, rigorous, and restrained. The work should feel confident and considered, with direct writing and evidence-led storytelling.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light editorial case-study system with expressive serif headings, compact sans-serif body copy, a focused blue accent, generous whitespace, and precise grid-based layouts. Avoid generic card-heavy presentation, decorative gradients, excessive motion, and visual effects that compete with the product evidence.

### Design Principles

1. Establish the problem completely before introducing the solution.
2. Use real screens and concise annotations as primary evidence.
3. Keep Path-maker, Inside Class, and Problem Solving structurally consistent while preserving the specific story of each surface.
4. Make hierarchy and reading order clear at a glance.
5. Maintain accessible contrast, responsive layouts, and restrained motion.
