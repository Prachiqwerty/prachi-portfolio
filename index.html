Pilot

Brochure Studio

Every programme brochure used to start from a blank file. Someone read the whole proposal, pulled out the facts, chased logos and laid out each page by hand. Now a designer uploads the proposal and answers only what the document leaves out. They pick a design family, and an agent writes, designs and checks every page. The draft opens in an editor where the designer finishes the copy and images, and every change is tracked.

  • One upload replaced the brief, the copy pass and the first layout
  • A designed draft in 8–12 minutes · a 20-page brochure for IIM Tiruchirappalli
  • 97 facts traced to their source · 0 facts guessed · gaps marked "To confirm"
  • 10 design families, built and tested by design · 0 engineering hours
Live

Varsity page automation

Every Varsity program page used to need someone to sit through the same long chat with Claude. Now one Slack form starts the work: a scheduled runner picks up the request, drafts all 20 sections into Storyblok from the brochure, and posts each step in the thread. People step in only to review and approve.

  • One Slack form replaced the repeated requirements chat
  • Picked up and drafting within 10 minutes, no chat to run
  • 20 sections from the brochure · 0 facts guessed · 0 pages auto-published
  • Built and tested by design · 0 engineering hours
